Model assumptions

  • AI-supported reading cuts workload by about 44% at full adoption and finds about 29% more cancers without more false positives (Hernström et al., 2025).
  • Without local validation only about half the benefit is assumed and false alarms rise from 6% to 18% of scans (Wong et al., 2021).
  • If the AI is wrong and the doctor stops checking, the error reaches the patient; over-reliance grows monthly unless sign-off and training slow it (Goddard et al., 2012).
  • Without a bias audit the AI misses about 35% more findings in under-represented patients, versus about 8% with one (Seyyed-Kalantari et al., 2021). In the doctor’s desk, patients aged 70+ stand in for the under-represented group.
  • A healthcare data breach costs $9.77M on average; safeguards cut the chance by 70% and the damage by 40% (IBM Security, 2024).
  • Legal exposure rises with AI-influenced errors; a liability policy lowers it (Price et al., 2019).
  • Conditions have costs: sign-off adds time, phasing delays benefits, safeguards and training add spending. Costs and event timing are our own illustrative assumptions.
