What is evidence and what is assumed

From the sources: 44.3% workload cut with similar detection (Lång et al., 2023); 82% to 45.5% and below 20% (Dratsch et al., 2023). The simulator uses the cited figures, not the 44.7% that appears on slides 3 and 10.

Illustrative assumptions (not findings): a 5% baseline miss rate, a 4% AI error rate, the AI being less accurate for one patient group (94% against 78% in the doctor’s desk), blind trust rising over time without condition 3, busy hours raising it, the size of each condition’s effect, event timing, and all costs. Costs are relative units, not dollars.
