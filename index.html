Model assumptions (what each number is based on)
  • AI-supported reading cuts radiologist workload by about 44% at full adoption, and finds about 29% more cancers without more false positives (Hernström et al., 2025). Auto-approve is assumed slightly faster still.
  • Vendor-reported accuracy often does not hold locally. Without local validation, only about half of the expected benefit is assumed, and false alarms rise from 6% to 18% of scans (Wong et al., 2021).
  • If the AI is wrong and the doctor has stopped checking, the error reaches the patient. Over-reliance grows monthly unless sign-off and training slow it (Goddard et al., 2012).
  • Without a bias audit, the AI misses about 35% more findings in under-represented patients, versus about 8% with one (Seyyed-Kalantari et al., 2021).
  • A breach costs $9.77M on average in healthcare; safeguards are assumed to cut the damage by 40% and the chance by 70% (IBM Security, 2024).
  • Legal exposure rises with AI-influenced errors; a liability policy lowers it (Price et al., 2019).
  • Conditions have costs: sign-off adds doctor time, a phased rollout delays benefits, and safeguards and training add spending. Cost figures and event timing are our own illustrative assumptions.
